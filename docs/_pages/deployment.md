PhotoDB and AudioDB are twin platforms, contained in one application. Depending on your data (image, audio or both) you can choose to utilize both platforms simultanously or utilize only one of the two. Since both platforms are contained in one application the installation process is the same for users intending to use only PhotoDB, only AudioDB, or both. 

PhotoDB / AudioDB is available for [Ubuntu](/photodb/deployment/deployment_ubuntu.html) or [Windows](/photodb/deployment/deployment_windows.html).

To use PhotoDB / AudioDB you will need **Java 11** or newer.
